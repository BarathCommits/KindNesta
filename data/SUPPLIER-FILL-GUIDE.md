# Supplier guideline — KindNesta product template

Share **`partner-product-template.csv`** with suppliers. Open in Google Sheets or Excel. Keep the header row. **One row = one SKU.**

All columns are **mandatory**. Attach product photos (and any certificates you already have) in the same email.

---

## Columns

| Column | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| `sku` | Your article / SKU code |
| `title` | Clear product name |
| `brand` | Your company / brand |
| `category` | Free text (e.g. packaging, home, personal-care, other) |
| `description` | What it is and who it is for |
| `materials` | What it is made of |
| `image` | Main photo filename |
| `publicClaims` | Exact wording KindNesta may publish — or `none` |
| `contactEmail` | Who we should reply to |

**Claim rule:** no generic *eco-friendly / green / sustainable / climate neutral* without proof. Prefer specific claims (e.g. scheme name + %).

---

## What to send

1. Completed `partner-product-template.csv`  
2. Product photos named as in `image`  
3. Any certificates you already hold (optional)

Email: **kindnesta@proton.me**  
Subject: `KindNesta product submission — [Brand] — [Date]`

KindNesta will follow up only for extra evidence needed to list and score the product.
