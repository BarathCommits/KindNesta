What the score is (and is not)

The Kindness Score is KindNesta’s internal comparison tool. It is not a third-party life-cycle assessment, PEF study, or official EU ecolabel. We show it so buyers can see how much verifiable evidence sits behind a product story.

Why Europe shapes the rules

  • EmpCo (Directive 2024/825) — from 27 Sep 2026, generic “eco/green/sustainable” claims and many self-made sustainability labels face strict limits.
  • PPWR (EU 2025/40) — packaging recyclability, recycled content, and waste prevention expectations.
  • REACH — chemical safety and substances of very high concern.
  • ESPR / Digital Product Passport — growing demand for composition, origin, and circularity data (textiles are a priority).

How scoring works

  1. Gate checks (A1–A7) — claim hygiene, REACH baseline, evidence age, trade-off honesty. Any Fail caps the score at 49 and blocks weak claims.
  2. Evidence pillars (B1–B6) — chemicals, material %, certifications, circularity, shipped packaging, traceability.
  3. KindNesta review — we confirm documents before treating a score as verified.

Do products need certificates to appear here?

To be listed: basic product data and honest descriptions. Chemical compliance expectations still apply for EU-bound goods.

To make green claims or earn Strong scores: yes — claim-specific third-party proof. Typical schemes:

  • GRS / RCS — recycled content chain of custody
  • GOTS — organic textiles
  • FSC / PEFC — responsible paper / forest fibre
  • OEKO-TEX Standard 100 — harmful substance testing for textiles
  • EU Ecolabel / Blue Angel — broader “excellent performance” labels

What buyers should check on a product page

  • Score band + notes (why the number is what it is)
  • Specific bullets (materials, certifications named)
  • Product details table (materials, dimensions, browse node)
  • Variant options under the same parent product

Provisional scores

Some catalogue scores began from supplier price-list text before full evidence packs arrived. Treat those as provisional until certificates, BOM %, and REACH statements are verified against our checklist.
