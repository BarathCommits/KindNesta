Process

How to submit products

  1. 1

    Download the template

    Get the CSV and fill guide. Open the CSV in Google Sheets or Excel.

  2. 2

    Fill one row per SKU

    Put company name and date on rows 1–2, then complete listing, gates, and score columns. Write N/A where a field truly does not apply.

  3. 3

    Attach evidence

    Photos and certificate / evidence PDFs — filenames must match the sheet.

  4. 4

    Send to KindNesta

    Email the CSV + files to kindnesta@proton.me. We acknowledge within 2 business days and confirm the Kindness Score.

Requirements

What we need from suppliers

Certificates are not required just to be listed — but they are required if you want recycled / organic / FSC-style claims or a Strong Kindness Score.

Listing fields

SKU, title, category, description, materials, image, public claims, and contact email — plus company name and date at the top of the sheet.

Gate checks A1–A7

Pass / Fail / N/A on claim hygiene, REACH baseline, evidence on file, and trade-off honesty. Any Fail caps the score at 49.

Score evidence

Materials %, certificates, chemicals, circularity, shipped packaging, and traceability — use N/A only when the field does not apply to that product.

Evidence files

Attach photos plus the PDFs named in the certificate and evidence columns.

EU claim rules (read before writing copy)

Under EmpCo (from 27 Sep 2026 in the EU), generic words like eco-friendly,green, sustainable, or climate neutral face strict limits without excellent, verified performance. Prefer specific claims with % and scheme names.

  • Good: Made from 100% GRS-certified recycled cotton paper
  • Bad: Eco-friendly hangtag

Typical proof by claim type:

  • Recycled content → GRS or RCS
  • Organic cotton → GOTS
  • Responsible paper / forest fibre → FSC or PEFC
  • Harmful-substance testing (textiles) → OEKO-TEX Standard 100
  • Broad excellent performance → EU Ecolabel / Blue Angel
