    Download the pack

  2. 2

    Fill one row per SKU

    Keep the header row. Replace example rows. Use the same parentId for variants.

  3. 3

    Attach evidence

  4. 4

    Send to KindNesta

    Email the completed CSV + evidence pack to kindnesta@proton.me. We acknowledge within 2 business days, complete an initial gate review within ~10 business days, then confirm the score and list the product.

Requirements

What we need from suppliers

Certificates are not required just to be listed — but they are required if you want recycled / organic / FSC-style claims or a Strong Kindness Score.

Honest product data

Clear title, materials, dimensions, images, and specific public claims only — no generic “eco-friendly” fluff.

Evidence for green claims

If you claim recycled, organic, FSC, etc., attach matching certificates with numbers, scope, and expiry dates.

Chemical baseline

REACH compliance answers for EU-bound goods. SVHC disclosure if above threshold. OEKO-TEX or equivalent when relevant.

Traceability

Country of manufacture, tier-1 identity, main material origin, and batch-level traceability where available (DPP-ready).

Shipped packaging facts

Pack materials, weight, recycled plastic %, overpack justification, and sorting/label plan for PPWR-minded buyers.

Gate self-check

Complete A1–A7 on the template. Any FAIL caps the Kindness Score at 49 until fixed.

EU claim rules (read before writing copy)

Under EmpCo (from 27 Sep 2026 in the EU), generic words like eco-friendly,green, sustainable, or climate neutral face strict limits without excellent, verified performance. Prefer specific claims with % and scheme names.

  • Good: Made from 100% GRS-certified recycled cotton paper
  • Bad: Eco-friendly hangtag

Typical proof by claim type:

  • Recycled content → GRS or RCS
  • Organic cotton → GOTS
  • Responsible paper / forest fibre → FSC or PEFC
  • Harmful-substance testing (textiles) → OEKO-TEX Standard 100
  • Broad excellent performance → EU Ecolabel / Blue Angel
