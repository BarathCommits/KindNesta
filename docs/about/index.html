About

We source and sell kinder products

KindNesta is a startup that sources and sells eco-friendly products — so buyers can compare impact, pack economics, and assortment fit, then purchase with confidence.

For retail outlets

Specialty grocery, gift, zero-waste, and lifestyle retailers buy from KindNesta to fill shelves with products that have a clear Kindness Score and a merchandisable story.

  • Case-ready formats across kitchen, home, care, garden, and dress
  • Pack size, MOQ, and suggested retail on every product
  • Score notes for shelf talkers and staff training
  • Sales support for pricing, lead times, and samples

For enterprise buyers

Facilities, hospitality, and people-ops teams buy kinder essentials for workplaces, amenities, and employee programs — with scoring that supports vendor questionnaires.

  • Volume-friendly personal care and home care formats
  • Transparent inputs: materials, packaging, sourcing
  • Support for RFPs and multi-site programs
  • Sample requests before line review or rollout

How Kindness Score works

Every product we sell carries a Kindness Score from 0 to 100. Higher means a stronger profile for people and planet. Scores are set by the KindNesta team based on:

  • Materials and ingredients
  • Packaging and refill options
  • Sourcing and logistics signals we can verify

Scores are not a third-party life-cycle assessment. They are a transparent rating so you can compare products quickly. Product pages include the notes behind each number.
