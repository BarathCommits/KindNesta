How Kindness Score works

Every product carries a Kindness Score from 0 to 100. Higher means a stronger evidence profile for people and planet. Scores follow KindNesta’sEU-aligned checklist (chemical safety, material %, third-party certifications, circularity, packaging, and traceability) — informed by EmpCo green-claim rules, PPWR packaging expectations, REACH, and ESPR / Digital Product Passport readiness.

  • Gate checks stop weak or illegal-style green claims
  • Pillar checks award points only when evidence is on file
  • Recognised third-party certifications score higher than unverified claims

Scores are not a third-party life-cycle assessment. They are a transparent rating so you can compare products quickly.Read the full Kindness Score guide →
