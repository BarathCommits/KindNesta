About

The kinder way to buy.

KindNesta is a sustainability e-commerce destination — helping buyers discover kinder products and compare impact with a Kindness Score on every listing.

Who scores the products?

KindNesta reviews supplier submissions against our EU-aligned checklist (gates A1–A7 and evidence pillars). The Kindness Score is our internal comparison rating — not a third-party ecolabel, PEF study, or official EU mark. Until certificates and material evidence are verified, scores are marked provisional.

  • Transparent inputs: materials, packaging, certifications
  • Score notes for vendor questionnaires
  • Support for multi-site and program planning

How Kindness Score works

Every product carries a Kindness Score from 0 to 100. Higher means a stronger evidence profile. Scores follow KindNesta’s EU-aligned checklist — informed by EmpCo green-claim rules, PPWR packaging expectations, REACH, and ESPR / Digital Product Passport readiness.
