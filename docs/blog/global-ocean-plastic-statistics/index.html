Ocean plastics: what the headline statistics really mean

Million-tonne leakage estimates drive policy—but product claims still need local proof.

Key figures

  • Scientific estimates of annual plastic emissions to oceans are in the million-tonne range
  • Macroplastics and microplastics behave differently
  • Recycling claims ≠ ocean cleanup claims

Global estimates of plastic entering aquatic environments are staggering and motivate PPWR-style laws and bag bans. Brands sometimes leap from those stats to product slogans (“ocean friendly”) without mechanism or measurement.

Rule for KindNesta Do not claim ocean benefit unless you have a specific, auditable intervention. Prefer material facts: recycled %, polymer type, recyclability design.
