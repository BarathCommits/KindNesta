East Africa plastic bag bans: policy that changed everyday retail

Kenya and neighbours showed how strict plastic bag bans can transform street-level packaging.

Key figures

  • Kenya’s bag ban among the world’s strictest
  • Regional ripple effects on retail carry bags
  • Enforcement intensity varies by country

Several East African countries restricted thin plastic carrier bags, with Kenya often cited for tough penalties. The result: rapid shifts to alternative bags—and a reminder that policy can move faster than voluntary eco-labels.

Exporters serving African retail should track national bag thickness and material bans separately from EU PPWR—the rules are not the same.
