Brazil: national waste policy meets Amazon supply-chain pressure

Solid waste policy and deforestation due-diligence expectations reshape commodity sourcing narratives.

Key figures

  • National Solid Waste Policy frames EPR concepts
  • Amazon-linked commodities face EU deforestation rules
  • Packaging recycling rates vary widely by city

Brazil’s National Solid Waste Policy introduced EPR-style responsibilities, while international buyers increasingly scrutinise Amazon-linked deforestation risk for commodities. Even when a product is a hangtag or carton, brand due diligence may ask about pulp origin and FSC/PEFC chain of custody.

For paper packaging FSC or PEFC is the practical language global retailers understand.
