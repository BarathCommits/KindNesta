Canada combined product restrictions on certain single-use plastics with stronger attention to environmental claim substantiation via Competition Bureau guidance. Provincial EPR programs add another compliance layer for packaging.

Suppliers shipping into Canada should avoid absolute recyclability claims and keep certificate files ready for retail audits.
