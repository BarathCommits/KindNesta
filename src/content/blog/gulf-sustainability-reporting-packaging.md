Gulf economies are investing in sustainability strategies while luxury and tourism retail expand. That combination increases demand for certified paper packaging, refined hangtags, and clearer chemical compliance—often following EU brand playbooks even when local law differs.

Partners should still complete REACH and cert fields; GCC buyers of European brands usually expect the same dossier.
