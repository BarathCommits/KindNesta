Labels, tapes, and hangtags are small components—but they sit on garments responsible for large water and waste footprints upstream. GOTS, GRS, and OEKO-TEX exist because fibre and wet-processing impacts are real.

When KindNesta scores a label SKU, chemical pillar B1 and recycled/organic pillar B2 are not bureaucracy; they are how buyers avoid exporting harm while importing “green” aesthetics.
