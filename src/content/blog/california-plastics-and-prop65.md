California’s packaging and plastics agenda—including producer responsibility under SB 54—pushes brands toward recyclable formats and reduced plastic. Separately, Proposition 65 can require warnings when listed chemicals are present above safe-harbor levels.

**Supplier action**
Disclose intentional chemistry early. OEKO-TEX or equivalent testing helps, but Prop 65 is its own list—do not assume one cert covers everything.
