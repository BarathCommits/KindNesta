South Korea’s extended producer responsibility system for packaging has operated for years, pairing producer fees with collection and recycling infrastructure. That maturity means “recyclable” claims are easier to stress-test against real sorting systems.

When Korean mills claim recyclability for export goods, request the same evidence EU PPWR will eventually demand: separable components and dominant material clarity.
