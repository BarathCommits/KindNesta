The IPCC’s Sixth Assessment Report reinforces that human-caused climate change is already intensifying extremes. For packaging and textile trims, that means cotton yields, energy costs, and shipping reliability are climate variables—not background noise.

Sustainability scoring that only celebrates recycled logos while ignoring resilience and truthful claims misses the point. Evidence-based materials + honest marketing is the baseline.
