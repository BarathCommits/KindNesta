ESPR creates a horizontal ecodesign framework and enables Digital Product Passports (DPPs). Exact textile fields will land in delegated acts, but the direction is clear: composition, chemicals, durability, and traceability become product data—not brochure fluff.

**Collect now**
- Fibre/material composition with percentages
- Country of manufacture and material origin
- Substances of concern / REACH references
- Care and end-of-life guidance
- Tier-1 supplier identity
