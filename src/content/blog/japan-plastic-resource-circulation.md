Japan’s Plastic Resource Circulation Act encourages businesses to design products and packaging that are easier to circulate—reducing unnecessary plastics and improving recycling pathways. Clear material identification on packaging aligns with both Japanese retail norms and EU PPWR thinking.

**Buyer tip**
Ask Japanese suppliers for material marks and recycled-content declarations with test methods, not only marketing copy.
