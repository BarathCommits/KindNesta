Nordic municipalities and agencies are experienced circular buyers. Tenders may reference Nordic Swan, EU Ecolabel, or detailed restricted substance lists. For KindNesta-style catalogues, Strong Kindness Scores with real certs travel well in these markets.

If you only have unverified “recycled look” paper, expect to lose Nordic tenders.
