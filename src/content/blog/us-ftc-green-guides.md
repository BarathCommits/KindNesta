In the United States, the Federal Trade Commission’s Green Guides explain how terms like “recyclable”, “recycled”, and “compostable” should be qualified. Overstating recycled content or recyclability where facilities are scarce is a classic enforcement risk.

**For global catalogues**
If a product is sold into both EU and US channels, use the stricter substantiation: certificate + %, and avoid absolute recyclability claims unless infrastructure is widely available.
