The Australian Packaging Covenant Organisation (APCO) set widely referenced 2025 targets: packaging that is reusable, recyclable, or compostable, with rising recycled content. Progress varies by polymer and collection access—reminding buyers that recyclable design ≠ recycled everywhere.
